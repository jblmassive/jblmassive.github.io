Stage management, multicam editing and branded clips in one macOS app.

  • In development

Multicam

Bring all the recorded angles from a set into one project, sync them to a shared audio track, and cut between cameras to make highlights and set recaps.

  1. Sync the angles

    Line up camera footage against the set audio.

  2. Cut the highlights

    Choose angles and turn the best moments into clips or a recap.

  3. Brand and export

    Finish the edit in Social Studio and export it for social media.

Social Studio

Preview clips, adjust audio levels, add your logo and export in several sizes. Post Builder brings photos and videos together in one edit.

Stage Management

We’re building Stage Command to keep the running order, artist requirements and stage handovers together. When a set runs long, we want to see what that means for the next act and how much time we have before curfew.

What’s working today

Build the running order

Schedule artists and changeovers, assign stages and risers, and keep equipment notes and riders with each act.

Track what happens live

Log actual starts and finishes, check setup readiness, and keep track of requests and issues during the show.

See the impact of an overrun

Preview changes to later slots while respecting fixed starts, minimum set lengths and the show curfew.

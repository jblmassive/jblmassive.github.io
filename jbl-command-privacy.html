JBL Command Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

Who operates JBL Command

JBL Command is operated by Jungle Book Legion Ltd. For privacy questions and requests, contact privacy@junglebooklegion.com.

What data JBL Command handles

When you connect a service, JBL Command uses the account details and permissions needed for the features you enable. For Instagram, this can include your professional account ID and username, sender and conversation identifiers, timestamps, and message content sent to your business account.

If you connect Microsoft Outlook or Google Calendar, JBL Command uses mailbox or calendar data to display email and schedules and carry out actions you choose, such as archiving an email or updating a calendar event.

Why we use this data

We use connected account data to sign you in, display and manage business communications and schedules, and troubleshoot connection failures.

Where data is processed

Meta, Microsoft and Google process data through their respective services when connected. The Instagram integration uses Cloudflare to receive webhook events and store messages for display in JBL Command. JBL Command uses Apple Keychain to store Instagram sign-in tokens on your device.

How long we keep data

  • Instagram messages: JBL Command stores up to the latest 100 messages per conversation in Cloudflare KV. New messages displace older messages above that limit. Stored conversation data has no automatic time-based expiry.
  • Diagnostic logs: The Instagram integration can log incoming webhook payloads, including message content and identifiers. If Cloudflare Workers Logs is enabled, the Workers Free plan retains those logs for up to three days.
  • Local connection data: Instagram sign-in credentials are stored in Apple Keychain on your device. You can revoke the provider grant through your account settings.

Removing a provider’s authorization stops future access through that grant. Copies already stored for JBL Command are handled separately.

Your choices and rights

You can revoke JBL Command’s access in your connected provider’s account settings. Contact privacy@junglebooklegion.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data held for JBL Command.

Depending on applicable law, you may also have the right to restrict or object to processing, request a portable copy of your data, or withdraw consent where processing relies on it. You can lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In Ireland, that is the Data Protection Commission.
